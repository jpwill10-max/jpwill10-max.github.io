What Is This App?

The Historical Civilization Guide is designed to help users learn about different civilizations throughout history.

Instead of searching through many different websites, users can choose a historical era and explore civilizations that existed during that time.

Each civilization will provide information about its time period, location, government, culture, religion, achievements, and other important parts of its history.
