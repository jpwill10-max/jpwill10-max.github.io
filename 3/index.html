Historical Eras & Civilizations

Project 2 Web App Proposal

Topic Description

Historical eras and civilizations are important for understanding how societies developed, changed, and influenced the world we live in today. A historical civilization is a society with its own systems of government, culture, technology, religion, economy, and social structure. Studying civilizations from different time periods can help people understand how people lived and how ideas, technologies, and traditions changed over time. However, someone who is new to history may have difficulty knowing where to start because there is a large amount of historical information available, often spread across many different pages and resources. My web application will provide a simpler, more organized way for users to explore major civilizations based on the historical era they are interested in.

There are already several websites that provide educational information about history. For example, World History Encyclopedia organizes historical information by time period, region, and subject. It includes resources about civilizations from prehistoric, ancient, medieval, early modern, and modern periods. Khan Academy also provides a World History course covering topics such as early civilizations, ancient empires, the Middle Ages, and the early modern period. While these resources provide a large amount of useful information, my application will focus on making exploration simpler and more interactive. Users will first select a historical era and then select a civilization within that era. The application will then present consistent information about that civilization, allowing users to explore history without having to search through large amounts of information themselves.

App Description

My proposed web application will be an interactive guide to historical eras and civilizations. The Home page will introduce users to the topic and explain why learning about historical civilizations is useful for understanding how societies developed over time. The page will include a brief introduction to the website, historical images, and a simple explanation of how the interactive portion works. I also plan to include navigation links that allow users to move between the Home page, the interactive civilization guide, the additional history information page, and the Contact Us page.

Interactive Civilization Guide

The second page will contain the main interactive feature of the application. Users will make two selections in sequence. The first selection will determine which four civilization choices are displayed during the second step.

  1. Choose a Historical Era
    • Ancient Era
    • Classical Era
    • Medieval Era
    • Early Modern Era
  2. Choose a Civilization
    • Ancient Era: Ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, Indus Valley Civilization, Shang China
    • Classical Era: Ancient Greece, Roman Empire, Han Dynasty, Maya Civilization
    • Medieval Era: Byzantine Empire, Medieval Europe, Mali Empire, Feudal Japan
    • Early Modern Era: Ottoman Empire, Mughal Empire, Renaissance Italy, Tokugawa Japan
  3. View Civilization Information

    After the user selects an era and civilization, the application will display information about the selected civilization. Each result will use the same format and include the civilization's time period, geographic location, map, government or political structure, culture or religion, major achievements, and historical images.

The two selections will create 16 possible combinations. Keeping the information in the same format for all 16 choices will make the application easier to use and will allow the same JavaScript structure to be reused later.

Additional History Information

The third page will provide additional historical information that does not require user interaction. This page could explain topics such as how historians divide history into different eras, how civilizations developed, how trade and migration connected different societies, and how historical sources are used to understand the past. This information will give users additional context without repeating the civilization information from the interactive page.

Contact Us

The fourth page will be a Contact Us page. Initially, this page will be static and will allow users to enter information such as their name, email address, subject, and message. Users could use the form to provide feedback about the website, report an issue, or suggest another historical civilization that they would like to see added. Later in the course, this information could be connected to the database so that submitted messages can be stored and managed.
