Jake Sully

This is Jake Sully from the movie Avatar. He was a human soldier sent to another planet to communicate with the indigenous people of the planet. He ends up joining them to make the destructive humans leave the planet.

I like this character for theses reasons:

He decides to go against every human there to protect the indigenous people of the plant, doing this while being in the human military base.

He is a hardcore marine but is still intelligent enough to realize the beauty and culture of the planet, eventually becoming one of them

He is a protector. In the beginning of the movie he fights a guy harrassing a bartender, keep in mind Jake is in a wheelchair unable to walk.
